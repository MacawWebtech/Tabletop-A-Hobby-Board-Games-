  • Same-day in Chennai if ordered before 2 pm

On your turn you do one thing: draw cards, claim a route, or take new destination tickets. That simplicity makes it the game we recommend most often to families moving beyond Ludo and Monopoly.

Longer routes score more, and completed tickets earn big bonuses at the end, so there's always a reason to take a risk.

  • Board map
  • 225 plastic trains in 5 colours
  • 144 illustrated cards
  • 5 scoring markers and rulebook

Complexity 2 of 5 (easy). Most people learn it in one practice round. Great for families and first-timers.

Best for groups who enjoy social, competitive and relaxed games. Plays with 2–5 people, ages 8 and up.
