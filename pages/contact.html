Orders, delivery and returns

Frequently asked questions

How fast is delivery?

Chennai orders placed before 2 pm are delivered the same evening. Elsewhere in India, tracked courier takes 2–5 working days. Store pickup is ready in 2 hours.

How much does delivery cost?

Chennai same-day is ₹99, free over ₹1,500. Pan-India courier is ₹149, free over ₹2,500. Pickup is always free.

How do I pay?

After we confirm your order on WhatsApp, pay by UPI or card link. Cash on delivery is available in Chennai, and you can pay at the counter for pickups.

Can I return a game?

Unopened games can be returned within 7 days for a full refund. Damaged or incomplete games are replaced free. Send us a photo and we'll arrange it.

Can you get a game you don't list?

Usually, yes. We order from Indian distributors weekly. Send an enquiry with the game's name and we'll give you a price and arrival date.
