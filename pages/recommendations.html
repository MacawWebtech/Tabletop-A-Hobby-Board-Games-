Choosing questions

Common questions before buying

Can I play a game before I buy it?

Yes. Our table library has 300+ open copies. Drop in during store hours or join a game night and staff will teach you any game on the shelves.

Are the age ratings strict?

They're a guide to reading and rules, not content. Children a year or two younger often manage fine when paired with an adult.

What if we don't like the game?

Unopened games can be returned within 7 days. If you've opened it and it isn't right for your group, bring it in and we'll offer store credit on a case-by-case basis.

Do games come in Tamil or Hindi editions?

Some party and word games do. Ask us about a specific game and we'll check which language editions our distributors carry.

Still unsure? Talk to a game advisor.

Tell us about your group and we'll suggest games by phone, WhatsApp or email. Free, with no obligation to buy.
