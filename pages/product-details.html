Wingspan puts you in the role of a bird enthusiast — researcher, bird watcher, ornithologist — seeking to discover and attract the best birds to your network of wildlife reserves. Each bird extends a chain of powerful combinations in one of your habitats: forest, grassland, or wetland.

Designed by Elizabeth Hargrave, Wingspan was built to appeal equally to experienced strategy gamers and people who have never played a hobby game before — a rare combination that's made it a modern classic.

  • 170 unique bird cards
  • 103 food tokens
  • 75 egg miniatures
  • 26 bonus cards, 16 automa cards
  • 5 player mats, 1 birdfeeder dice tower
  1. Each round, choose one action: play a bird, gain food, lay eggs, or draw bird cards.
  2. Chain bird powers together across your forest, grassland and wetland rows.
  3. Score points for birds played, eggs laid, food cached and round-end goals.
  4. After four rounds, the highest total score wins.
