Built Around the Joy of Playing Together.

Tabletop & Co. started as a folding table at a weekend market and grew into a store — but the reason for opening the doors every morning hasn't changed: get more people around a table, playing something they love.

Our story

From a market table
to a home for players.

2016

The folding table

Two friends, a crate of secondhand games, and a Sunday market stall in Mylapore.

2018

First storefront

A 400 sq ft shop with three shelves, one demo table, and a lot of borrowed chairs.

2021

Game nights begin

What started as an after-hours favour for regulars became a weekly fixture with a waitlist.

Today

1,200+ games

A full hobby store carrying strategy, family, party and RPG titles — still recommended by hand.

What we believe

Play Without Limits

Whether you have ten minutes or all night, there's a game for the time you actually have.

Discover Something New

We play everything we sell, so recommendations come from experience, not a sales chart.

Everyone Has a Seat

First-timers get taught the rules table-side. No one sits out because they don't know how to play yet.

Community Comes First

The store exists to get people around a table together — sales are how we keep the lights on for that.
