Our story

Started around one kitchen table

Karthik started Tabletop after years of carrying board games back from trips abroad for friends. Chennai had players, but nowhere to find or try modern games.

We still work the same way. Every game on our shelves has been played by someone on staff, so when you ask whether it suits your family, you get an answer from experience, not a product description.

How we run the shop

We play before we sell

No game goes on the shelf until at least two staff members have played it with a real group.

Honest advice

Packed like it's ours
