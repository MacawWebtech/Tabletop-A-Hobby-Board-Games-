How to choose

Read any game in five seconds

Every game in our shop shows the same four facts, like the side of a game box. Match them to your group and you'll rarely pick wrong.

  • Players

    The number of people the game works for. For big groups, look for a maximum of 5 or more.

  • Play time

    A typical game once everyone knows the rules. Add 15 minutes for the first game.

  • Age

    The youngest age that can play without help. Younger kids can often join as a team.

  • Complexity

    One dot is learn-in-a-minute. Five dots means a rulebook evening. Most families enjoy.
